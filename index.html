life-os

A personal, single-user app.

life-os is a private tool its owner runs on their own computer to keep track of their own finances, shopping and travel. It is not a service, it is not offered to anyone else, and it has no accounts or sign-ups.

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's own Gmail, read-only, to pick up bank alerts, card statements and receipts, and files them in a database on that same computer.
