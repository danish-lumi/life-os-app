Privacy policy

life-os · last updated 14 November 2026

life-os is a personal app with exactly one user, its owner, who runs it on their own computer. This page describes what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

Only the Gmail accounts its owner connects, with the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It searches only for messages from senders the owner listed (their banks, card issuers, shops and airlines). It never sends, deletes or changes mail.

Where the data goes

  • Matching messages and attachments are stored in a database and folder on the owner's computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server run by life-os; there is none.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • To read amounts and dates out of a message, its text may be passed to an AI model the owner chose and pays for (Anthropic's Claude, through the owner's own Claude Code account). Nothing else is shared with anyone.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Keeping and removing data

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored messages stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them.
